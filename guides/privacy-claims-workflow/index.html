A privacy claim should describe a workflow, not an entire domain

How to phrase a narrow product workflow claim with a checkable scope and a current source link.

Describe the exact activity

A useful claim names what the reader can verify, such as a browser-side editing step or a stated upload flow. Avoid expanding one observed behavior into a claim about every service, device or future version.

Say what the claim does not establish

A short scope note is helpful when a page does not independently prove retention policies, every integration or every operational setting. It keeps a practical explanation from becoming an untestable assurance.

Link to the current product page

Interfaces and policies can change. Point readers to the public page that describes the current workflow, and update the guide when that source changes instead of relying on a detached marketing sentence.

Final check

Open the exported result or use it at the intended size before approving it. Interfaces and options can change, so confirm the current workflow on the linked public tool page.
